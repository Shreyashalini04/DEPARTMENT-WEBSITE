WELCOME

About the Department

The Department of Economics is a well-established department of Sri Venkateswara College. It has an active academic environment, supportive faculty and an engaged student community.

The department encourages students to understand economic ideas, analyse real-world issues and take part in academic and co-curricular activities.

The undergraduate programme follows a four-year structure, with a research-oriented pathway in the final year.
